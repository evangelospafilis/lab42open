# 🎓 Master’s Thesis Opportunities

At **lab42open**, we bridge the gap between big biological data and actionable ecological knowledge. We develop open-source tools, deploy containerized workflows, and build text-mining pipelines to understand molecular ecology and biodiversity.

We are looking for motivated Master’s students (from Bioinformatics, Computer Science, Data Science, or Biology backgrounds) to tackle real-world computational challenges with us.
